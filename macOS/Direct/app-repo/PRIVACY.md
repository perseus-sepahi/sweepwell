*Effective 30 October 2026*

Sweepwell is made by Perseus Sepahi, an independent developer. This policy covers the Sweepwell app for macOS.

## The short version

Sweepwell does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it scans and everything it finds stays on your Mac.

## What stays on your Mac

- What Sweepwell finds when it scans your files, apps and startup items. It is never uploaded.
- Your settings, the running total of space you have swept, when your free trial started, and your licence key if you buy Sweepwell Pro.

To scan other apps' folders, Sweepwell asks for Full Disk Access, which you grant in System Settings. It uses that access only on your Mac.

## When Sweepwell connects to the internet

- **Trial users:** never. The trial works entirely offline.
- **After you buy Pro:** about once a week, Sweepwell downloads a public list of refunded licences from my licence service (a Cloudflare Worker) and checks your licence against it on your Mac. Nothing about you or your licence is sent. Like any internet connection, the request carries your IP address, which Cloudflare can see. I do not log it.
- **Tools you already have:** Storage Audit asks tools installed on your Mac, such as Xcode's simulator tool, Ollama or Homebrew, what they have stored. If you remove an item, the tool that owns it does the removing.

## Purchases

Sweepwell Pro is sold through Stripe, which handles the payment, your card details and sales tax. I never see your card number. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the checkout. Stripe tells me what was bought and the details you enter at checkout, such as your email address, which I use only to help with your purchase or a refund.

After you pay, my licence service asks Stripe to confirm the purchase and shows you a licence key. The service keeps only the purchase IDs of refunded or disputed orders, so that those keys stop working.

## Children

Sweepwell is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
