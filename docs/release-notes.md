The first public release of Sweepwell, free and open source under the MIT licence.

**What it does:** finds what is filling your Mac's disk, tells you what removing it costs, and removes only what you pick. System Junk, Large & Old Files, Duplicates, Space Lens, Storage Audit, Uninstaller, Startup & Activity, Maintenance, Shredder, and a menu bar panel with live readings.

**Install:** open `Sweepwell.dmg` and drag Sweepwell to Applications. Then turn on Sweepwell in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Full Disk Access so scans can see everything.

**Requirements:** macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Privacy:** no accounts, no analytics and no network connections. What Sweepwell finds stays on your Mac.
