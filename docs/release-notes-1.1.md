**New in 1.1:** the menu bar panel shows CPU, GPU, RAM and processor temperature side by side, each green, yellow, orange or red by how hard it is working. The GPU reading is new. Hover over the strip to see what each colour means.

**Install:** open `Sweepwell.dmg` and drag Sweepwell to Applications, replacing the old copy. Your settings and Full Disk Access carry over.

**Requirements:** macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon. Signed and notarized by Apple. Free and open source under the MIT licence.
